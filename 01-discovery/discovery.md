#Week 1 — Initial Discovery
## 1. Facts
1. Bookings are managed using a mixture of email spreadsheets and informal arrangements
2. Equipment can be double booked
3. Staff spend time checking bookings manually
4. Equipment may not be returned when expected
5. Students are not always sure who to contact about equipment
## 2. Assumptions
1. All equipment items will be uniquely identifiable
2. Users will have valid login credentials
3. Staff will have access to an administrative view
4. The system will be accessed via a web browser or app
## 3. Unknowns
1. Who is allowed to book equipment
2. How far in advance can bookings be made
3. What happens if equipment is returned late or damaged
4. Who maintains and updates the equipment records
## 4. Stakeholders
1. Students
2. Lecturer
3. IT Technicians
4. Management
5. IT Devlopers
## 5. Goals
1. Eliminate double-booking of equipment
2. Reduce staff time spent on manual booking administration
3. Improve accountability for equipment returns
## 6. Scope
1. Equipment can be double-booked
2. No reliable way to check availability
3. Manual, time-consuming administration
4. Lack of accountability for returns
## 7. Candidate Requirements
1. The system shall allow an authorised user to request equipment for a specified date and time.
2. The system shall prevent a booking from being confirmed if the equipment is already booked for an overlapping time slot.
3. The system shall display the real-time availability status of each piece of equipment to all authorised users.
4. The system shall send an automated reminder to the user when a piece of booked equipment is due to be returned.
